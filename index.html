About IronFit

Founded in 2018, IronFit Gym has grown into a premier fitness destination for athletes and beginners alike. We believe fitness is not just about looking good — it's about feeling strong, confident, and healthy.

Our state-of-the-art facility, expert coaches, and supportive community make IronFit the perfect place to start or elevate your fitness journey.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before joining IronFit.

What are the gym hours?
Our gym is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week for Pro and Elite members. Basic members have access from 6am to 10pm daily.
Do you offer trial memberships?
Yes! We offer a 7-day free trial for all new members. You'll get full access to the gym, classes, and a consultation with a trainer.
Are personal trainers available?
Absolutely. We have a team of certified personal trainers who can design a custom program for your goals. Sessions can be booked through our app or at the front desk.
What classes do you offer?
We offer a wide range of classes including Cardio Blast, Strength & Power, HIIT Express, Aqua Fit, and Cycle Rhythm. See our Classes section for details.
Can I cancel my membership anytime?
Yes, you can cancel your membership at any time with a 30-day notice. There are no hidden fees or long-term contracts.
